Cleo Test Management Reporting - Full Context
Overview

Cleo Test Management is a TypeScript monorepo that supports software delivery, test reporting, release reporting, governance and quality assurance activities for NHS-facing products and services.

The platform started as a test reporting solution but has evolved into a delivery intelligence and release management platform.

The long-term vision is:
